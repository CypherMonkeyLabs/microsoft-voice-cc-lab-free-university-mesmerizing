10 · Session border controllers

Cisco CUBE for Teams

CUBE is the usual bridge when CUCM is still in the building. It is still an SBC.

Room HTMLAll downloads

5 minutes · Visual · Example · Quiz · Sources · Ecosystem

Learn it in 5 minutes

Cisco Unified Border Element on a certified IOS-XE release peers TLS with the three commercial proxies, presents a certificate whose SAN matches the paired FQDN, and has dial-peers toward Teams and toward CUCM or the carrier. Teams expects early offer. A delayed-offer trunk from CUCM pushed all the way to Teams negotiates badly.

This campus does not paste a production dial-peer. Use Cisco’s current CUBE for Teams guide for the release you run. Trust the published signaling ranges rather than a single DNS answer. Offer a codec both sides share, G.711 at minimum unless you have a measured reason not to.

Use a certified CUBE release, early offer toward Teams, and a certificate name that matches the paired FQDN.

Worked example

CUBE is a certified SBC with dial-peers in two directions and early offer toward Teams.

  1. Cert Name matches FQDN
  2. Dial-peers Teams and CUCM
  3. Offer Early
  4. Codec Intersection not empty

If it fails: CUCM sends delayed offer all the way to Teams and calls fail with 488.

Quiz

Which description of Certified list changes is accurate?

The list of SBCs and contact-center solutions changes. A campus page is not the list. Quote the list the week you buy. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/direct-routing-border-controllers accessed 2026-10-14.

A colleague reports: A purchase order cites a 2022 PDF. Where do you start?

A purchase order cites a 2022 PDF. Link the design to the current Microsoft page and the firmware. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/direct-routing-border-controllers accessed 2026-10-14.

Which description of Session border controller is accurate?

The device that speaks SIP to Teams and SIP or another protocol to the carrier or PBX, and enforces the edge. Without a certified one, Direct Routing is unsupported. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/direct-routing-border-controllers accessed 2026-10-14.

Glossary

  • Early offer Teams Direct Routing presents media in the INVITE. An SBC or CUBE that waits for a delayed offer will negotiate badly. Config: Set the vendor profile for early offer toward Teams. Fault: CUCM sends delayed offer all the way to Teams.
  • AudioCodes A certified SBC family. Mediant devices and their management tools are common in Direct Routing designs. The Microsoft certified list is the support boundary, the vendor guide is the config. Config: Follow AudioCodes’ current Teams document for the firmware you run. Fault: A forum config from 2019 is pasted onto new firmware.
  • Ribbon SBC Ribbon’s certified session border controllers are a Direct Routing option. Core and software editions differ. Config: Use Ribbon’s current Teams guide and a public certificate. Fault: OPTIONS are answered on the wrong interface.
  • Oracle SBC Oracle Communications Session Border Controller can be certified for Direct Routing on specific releases. Check the Microsoft list for the release, not the logo. Config: Match the certified software version and the certificate name. Fault: The beautiful config is one release below the certified list.

Cross-platform bridges

CUCM

Cisco CUBE is one of the SBC families named in this lab’s certification lesson. A certified release can be the Direct Routing SBC between Unified CM and Teams.

The tenant still needs a verified domain on the SBC FQDN, and the user still needs Teams Phone. The studio translator does not open a live AXL session. Confirm the current certified release before you build.

Webex

Teams Phone and Webex Calling are separate clouds. This lab does not describe a feature that registers a Webex Calling user into a Teams tenant.

A number move is usually a carrier port, a certified SBC, or a meetings interop product. Confirm before relying.

UCCE

Unified CCE stays the ACD until you migrate it. Microsoft’s contact-center articles name Connect, Extend, and Unify. They do not name a UCCE connector in the notes this lab cites.

Voice into Teams is still Direct Routing or Operator Connect. The routing script stays on the contact-center product you bought. Confirm the vendor’s current model. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/teams-contact-center.

Future hooks

Copilot Studio, Azure Communication Services, and Graph are later tracks in this lab. Treat any uncited limit as confirm before relying.
