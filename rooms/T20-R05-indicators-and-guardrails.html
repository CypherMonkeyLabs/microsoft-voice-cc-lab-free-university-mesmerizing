Indicators and guardrails

A quality indicator produces a score. A guardrail produces a violation. The page treats them as different objects.

Room HTMLAll downloads

5 minutes · Visual · Example · Quiz · Ecosystem · Sources and go deeper

Learn it in 5 minutes

The quality and coaching page applies to Dynamics 365 Contact Center only. An evaluation plan sets the criteria, which conversations, the timing, and the actions. Frequency is In real time, during the conversation, or On conversation close, after it ends. Conditions pick the queue or workstream. Sampling is a percentage. Real-time actions can notify a supervisor, send a coaching nudge, or both, for ranges the page names Critical, Warning, and Normal. On conversation close, weights say how much each indicator contributes.

Indicators are questions with scores. Answer types on the page include Yes/No, multi-select, and single select. Guardrails are required or prohibited behaviors. They are evaluated for violations rather than numeric scores, and each has a priority of Low, Medium, or High. You need the Omnichannel Administrator role, and the page says to set up consumption-based billing and capacity. The page says not to use the feature for employment decisions, and not to infer emotional or psychological states. Build the library, then the plan, then activate it.

Quality evaluation uses scored indicators and unscored guardrails. Real time and on close are different frequencies. Do not use the score for employment decisions.

Worked example

An indicator scores. A guardrail flags a required or prohibited behavior. Neither one is an employment decision.

  1. Library Indicators and guardrails
  2. Plan Frequency, conditions, sampling
  3. Action Nudge, notify, or a score
  4. Limit Not an employment decision

If it fails: A prohibited phrase is given a numeric weight because it was entered as an indicator.

Quiz

Which description of Quality guardrail is accurate?

On the quality and coaching page, a guardrail defines a required or prohibited behavior. Guardrails are evaluated for violations rather than assigned numeric scores, and each has a priority. Indicators are the scored questions. A guardrail is not an indicator. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/contact-center/administer/configure-quality-coach accessed 2026-10-14.

A colleague reports: A prohibited behavior is given a numeric score because the indicator form was copied. Where do you start?

A prohibited behavior is given a numeric score because the indicator form was copied. Set priority and optional nudge text, then activate the guardrail before you add it to a plan. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/contact-center/administer/configure-quality-coach accessed 2026-10-14.

Which description of Short-term forecast is accurate?

The forecasting overview says a short-term scenario predicts volume and average handle time in 15-minute intervals for a horizon of up to six weeks (42 days), using a linear trend on internal or external data. Volume on that page excludes conversations that AI agents handle with no representative joining. Forecasting then feeds capacity planning, which drives scheduling. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/contact-center/use/workforce-management-forecast-overview accessed 2026-10-14.

Cross-platform bridges

CUCM

Cisco CUBE is one of the SBC families named in this lab’s certification lesson. A certified release can be the Direct Routing SBC between Unified CM and Teams.

The tenant still needs a verified domain on the SBC FQDN, and the user still needs Teams Phone. The studio translator does not open a live AXL session. Confirm the current certified release before you build.

Webex

Teams Phone and Webex Calling are separate clouds. This lab does not describe a feature that registers a Webex Calling user into a Teams tenant.

A number move is usually a carrier port, a certified SBC, or a meetings interop product. Confirm before relying.

UCCE

Unified CCE stays the ACD until you migrate it. Microsoft’s contact-center articles name Connect, Extend, and Unify. They do not name a UCCE connector in the notes this lab cites.

Voice into Teams is still Direct Routing or Operator Connect. The routing script stays on the contact-center product you bought. Confirm the vendor’s current model. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/teams-contact-center.

Future hooks

Copilot Studio, Azure Communication Services, and Graph are later tracks in this lab. Treat any uncited limit as confirm before relying.
