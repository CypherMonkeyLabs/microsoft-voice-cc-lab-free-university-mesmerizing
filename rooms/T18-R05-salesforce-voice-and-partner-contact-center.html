Salesforce Voice and Partner Contact Center

The old name is still in product screens. The pages opened for this room say what the current name is.

Room HTMLAll downloads

5 minutes · Visual · Example · Quiz · Ecosystem · Sources and go deeper

Learn it in 5 minutes

The help page opened for this room is titled Connect Voice from a Partner Contact Center. It says Salesforce Voice with Telephony Providers, formerly Service Cloud Voice, is now part of Partner Contact Center, and that you may still see the older name. Its learning map lists the Telephony Integration REST API, the Voice Toolkit API, and Lambda functions for Amazon Connect under Develop.

The same note still names Salesforce Voice with Amazon Connect, Salesforce Voice with Partner Telephony, and Salesforce Voice with Partner Telephony from Amazon Connect. It points to Agentforce Contact Center Voice for a built-in telephony offering. This room does not describe that offering beyond the pointer. A Salesforce developer-guide URL was requested during this build and did not open, so this room does not quote it.

Salesforce’s guide says Service Cloud Voice is now Salesforce Voice. The help page says that voice offering is now part of Partner Contact Center.

Quiz

Which description of Dynamics 365 voice channel is accurate?

The voice-channel page says it applies to Dynamics 365 Contact Center embedded, Contact Center standalone, and Customer Service. It provides native PSTN calling in Dynamics and says the channel is built on Azure Communication Services. Voice is routed with unified routing in the same way the page describes for an incoming chat. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/administer/voice-channel accessed 2026-10-14.

A colleague reports: Someone expects the Teams client because the number was bought in Teams. Where do you start?

Someone expects the Teams client because the number was bought in Teams. Read the capabilities table on that page. Do not copy the marketing section above it. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/administer/voice-channel accessed 2026-10-14.

Which description of Teams Phone in the voice channel is accurate?

The configure-Teams-Phone page says PSTN connectivity can be a Teams Calling Plan (service numbers only), Operator Connect, or Direct Routing, and that a Teams resource account is required for every number used with Dynamics 365 Contact Center. Representatives receive the call notification in the Copilot Service workspace app, on that page. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/contact-center/administer/configure-teams-phone-in-voice-channel accessed 2026-10-14.

Cross-platform bridges

CUCM

Cisco CUBE is one of the SBC families named in this lab’s certification lesson. A certified release can be the Direct Routing SBC between Unified CM and Teams.

The tenant still needs a verified domain on the SBC FQDN, and the user still needs Teams Phone. The studio translator does not open a live AXL session. Confirm the current certified release before you build.

Webex

Teams Phone and Webex Calling are separate clouds. This lab does not describe a feature that registers a Webex Calling user into a Teams tenant.

A number move is usually a carrier port, a certified SBC, or a meetings interop product. Confirm before relying.

UCCE

Unified CCE stays the ACD until you migrate it. Microsoft’s contact-center articles name Connect, Extend, and Unify. They do not name a UCCE connector in the notes this lab cites.

Voice into Teams is still Direct Routing or Operator Connect. The routing script stays on the contact-center product you bought. Confirm the vendor’s current model. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/teams-contact-center.

Future hooks

Copilot Studio, Azure Communication Services, and Graph are later tracks in this lab. Treat any uncited limit as confirm before relying.
