Learn it in 5 minutes

Classic topics are a menu you can test. Generative orchestration chooses a path and needs tighter bounds. Either way, ground the bot in knowledge sources you trust, and keep a human escape that does not depend on the model feeling cooperative.

Do not put this bot on the emergency number. Do not make it the only target of the public number unless overflow to a queue is real and tested. A keyword lab on this campus is a stand-in, not a model call.

Copilot Studio can front the voice channel if it is grounded and can always reach a human. Keep it off the emergency route.

Quiz

Which description of Teams interop with ACS is accurate?

ACS can be set up to call Teams users and to join Teams meetings when the interop prerequisites are met. A raw ACS number does not automatically become a Teams user. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/communication-services/concepts/call-automation/call-automation accessed 2026-10-14.

A colleague reports: The bot can call the PSTN and cannot transfer to a Teams expert. Where do you start?

The bot can call the PSTN and cannot transfer to a Teams expert. Follow the current interop article for the resource you created. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/communication-services/concepts/call-automation/call-automation accessed 2026-10-14.

Which description of Knowledge source is accurate?

The documents a Copilot or a generative agent is allowed to quote. If the source is wrong, the answer is wrong with confidence. Ground the agent or do not put it on the phone. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot-studio/fundamentals-what-is-copilot-studio accessed 2026-10-14.

Glossary

  • Call Automation The ACS server-side calling API. Your app answers, plays, recognizes DTMF or speech, and transfers. You receive events. You do not drive a desktop click. Config: Subscribe to the events and time out the recognize step. Fault: The app never answers, so the caller hears ringing until the platform gives up.
  • Calling SDK The ACS client SDK for a human in a browser or a mobile app. Different from Call Automation, which is the server. Config: Pick the SDK if a person is on the page. Pick automation if a bot answers. Fault: A web page uses the calling SDK and nobody handles the server events.
  • Recognize step The Call Automation action that collects DTMF or speech. It needs a timeout and a path for ‘none of the above.’ Config: Map 0 to a human queue and test it. Fault: The bot repeats the prompt until the caller hangs up.
  • Azure AI Speech Microsoft’s speech-to-text and text-to-speech service. Copilot Studio and custom IVRs can use it. It is not emergency calling. Config: Choose a voice you are allowed to use and log the recognition result. Fault: The prompt language does not match the caller and every intent is unknown.

Cross-platform bridges

CUCM

Cisco CUBE is one of the SBC families named in this lab’s certification lesson. A certified release can be the Direct Routing SBC between Unified CM and Teams.

The tenant still needs a verified domain on the SBC FQDN, and the user still needs Teams Phone. The studio translator does not open a live AXL session. Confirm the current certified release before you build.

Webex

Teams Phone and Webex Calling are separate clouds. This lab does not describe a feature that registers a Webex Calling user into a Teams tenant.

A number move is usually a carrier port, a certified SBC, or a meetings interop product. Confirm before relying.

UCCE

Unified CCE stays the ACD until you migrate it. Microsoft’s contact-center articles name Connect, Extend, and Unify. They do not name a UCCE connector in the notes this lab cites.

Voice into Teams is still Direct Routing or Operator Connect. The routing script stays on the contact-center product you bought. Confirm the vendor’s current model. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/teams-contact-center.

Future hooks

Copilot Studio, Azure Communication Services, and Graph are later tracks in this lab. Treat any uncited limit as confirm before relying.
