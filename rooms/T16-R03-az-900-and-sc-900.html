16 · Certifications and status

AZ-900 and SC-900

Fundamentals help with Azure and identity vocabulary. They do not make you a voice engineer.

Room HTMLAll downloads

5 minutes · Visual · Example · Quiz · Sources · Ecosystem

Learn it in 5 minutes

AZ-900, Azure Fundamentals, was not on the retirement list reviewed on 14 October 2026. SC-900, Security, Compliance, and Identity Fundamentals, is live and the English certification is scheduled to update on 21 October 2026. MS-900 retired on 31 March 2026. AI-900 retired on 30 June 2026.

Use AZ-900 if you need Azure words for ACS and Speech. Use SC-900 if you need Entra and compliance words for Conditional Access and recording’s trust boundary. Neither one replaces MS-700 or AB-250.

AZ-900 and SC-900 are optional context. They are not the voice exam or the contact-center exam.

Glossary

  • MS-700 MS-700 is still live, with a skills update on Oct 27 2026; it is NOT retired and has no replacement. It is the exam for Microsoft 365 Certified: Teams Administrator Associate. Config: Study the current guide, including network and call-quality objectives. There is no replacement exam. Fault: A candidate skips networking because they think the exam is only meetings.
  • MB-280 retired MB-280 retired Jul 31 2026, with AB-210 as its successor; AB-210 is in beta. It covered Sales and Customer Insights, not the contact-center product. Config: Do not book MB-280. AB-210 is its successor and is in beta. AB-250 is the contact-center exam. Fault: A job post requires MB-280 after the retirement date.
  • AB-210 Dynamics 365 Sales AI Consultant Associate. AB-210 is the successor to MB-280 and is in beta. It is not a replacement for MS-700. Config: Use AB-250 when the job is Dynamics 365 Contact Center. Use AB-210 only for a sales role, and remember it is in beta. Fault: Study time goes to sales Copilot for a queue-routing role.
  • AB-250 Transforming Contact Center Experiences with AI in Dynamics 365. It earns Dynamics 365 Contact Center AI Engineer Associate. Pass mark is 700. The public page allows 120 minutes. Config: Study deploy, channels, agents, work distribution, the representative experience, and analytics. Fault: A candidate revises MB-280 practice tests for this exam.

Cross-platform bridges

CUCM

Cisco CUBE is one of the SBC families named in this lab’s certification lesson. A certified release can be the Direct Routing SBC between Unified CM and Teams.

The tenant still needs a verified domain on the SBC FQDN, and the user still needs Teams Phone. The studio translator does not open a live AXL session. Confirm the current certified release before you build.

Webex

Teams Phone and Webex Calling are separate clouds. This lab does not describe a feature that registers a Webex Calling user into a Teams tenant.

A number move is usually a carrier port, a certified SBC, or a meetings interop product. Confirm before relying.

UCCE

Unified CCE stays the ACD until you migrate it. Microsoft’s contact-center articles name Connect, Extend, and Unify. They do not name a UCCE connector in the notes this lab cites.

Voice into Teams is still Direct Routing or Operator Connect. The routing script stays on the contact-center product you bought. Confirm the vendor’s current model. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/teams-contact-center.

Future hooks

Copilot Studio, Azure Communication Services, and Graph are later tracks in this lab. Treat any uncited limit as confirm before relying.
