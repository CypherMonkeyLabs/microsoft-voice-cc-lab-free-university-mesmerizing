Help

Menu

The Menu button is at the top left. The drawer slides in from the left. Esc or a tap outside closes it. On a phone the bottom bar is Menu, Learn, Diagrams, Play, Listen, and Guide.

Course

Start here is the short path. Tracks are the full course. Each room has a five-minute read, a drawing, an example, a quiz, sources, and an ecosystem panel.

Bot

The monkey greets you once, then sits at the right. Drag it. Ask it to open a room. Mic uses the browser, and the box is there if the mic is off. Read this page highlights the sentence it speaks.

Downloads

Every section has a colored download button. The library lists SVG, PNG, HTML, PDF, DOCX, JSON, PPTX, and one zip.
